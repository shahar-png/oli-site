faq · fair questions
So what exactly is the product?+
That's the one thing we keep for accepted testers — you'll see it on day one. What we'll say now: it changes how you take supplements, and it never sells you any.
Do I have to change what I take?+
No. We cover your current stack — the exact products and brands you already buy — at its normal monthly cost, for the 3 months of the alpha. We confirm the list with you at onboarding.
What does it cost me?+
Nothing, ever. The whole price is honest daily use and 10 minutes of feedback a week. And you can leave whenever — keep whatever's already been delivered.
What happens to my data?+
Used only to run the alpha and build the product. Never sold, never shared with supplement brands, deleted on request.
I'm on Android, or outside the US. Anything for me?+
Not in cohort 01 — but apply anyway and mark it in the form. You'll be first in line when the next cohorts open up.
When does it start?+
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; cohort 01 starts as soon as it's full. Selected testers hear from us within days.
